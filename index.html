Privacy Policy for Social QR Studio

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Social QR Studio is an application that allows users to generate and share QR codes for social media links.

Information You Enter

Users may enter social media links to generate QR codes. These links are used to create the requested QR codes.

Account Registration

Social QR Studio does not currently require users to create an account or log in.

Sharing QR Codes

Users can choose to share generated QR codes through other applications. Information shared through those applications is subject to their respective privacy policies.

Third-Party Services

Links entered by users may direct people to third-party websites or applications. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
